Vacuum math

The bin fills mid-room, the last bag went in three months ago, and the filter light has been on since spring. Vacuum math turns pickup per session into sessions per bin, a bag into bags and cost per year, and a filter's rated hours into the week it actually dies in your house. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Bin plan - bin size and pickup per session become sessions to full and empties per year.
  • Bag plan - a bag becomes sessions, bags a year, and the yearly cost.
  • Filter plan - rated filter hours and your pace become weeks per filter and the yearly spend.

Labeled norms

Labeled pickup: about 0.4 liters a session for a labeled weekly whole-home pass. Labeled filter life: about 50-100 run-hours on the maker's rating. These are reference values, not rules - your floors, pets and machine decide the real numbers, and the maker's manual decides the filter schedule.
